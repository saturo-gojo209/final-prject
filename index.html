about the jewels of the black phoenixes

Once upon a time in the magical land of Tania in the mighty fortress of the flaming sword or Narin Hamiya in the ancient tongue called it was. The bells were ringing.Not once but 7 times. Not for peace. Not for mourning. For war. The Lord delivered his speech, ”WE ARE UNDER SIEGE TODAY THE COURAGE OF OUR MEN SHALL BE SHOWN TO EVIL.FOR THE EMPEROR.” A massive cry of FOR THE EMPEROR for this was the battle cry of the last High King of the twin realms Ibrahimuis and Estania Thorvald vi. Across the horizon marched countless armies of orcs, goblins, trolls, cyclopes, and dragons, accompanied by nameless horrors from beyond the stars. Their numbers blotted out the distant hills, and the earth trembled beneath their advance. Before them stood the mighty fortress of Narin Hamiya, a colossal stronghold forged from pure, glimmering silverite, its walls reflecting the heavens beneath a sky of iron grey. Seven great gates guarded its outer defences, while towering ramparts stretched across the land and spires pierced the storm-laden clouds above. Upon its battlements stood countless nameless soldiers, clad in steel and shadow, gripping bows, swords, and spears with trembling hands and steadfast hearts. They gazed upon the approaching horde. And they knew that before the year was done, the stones beneath their feet would drink deeply of blood. The great black gatehouses thundered shut. Portcullises descended with a deafening crash, and the drawbridges rose from the earth, severing the fortress from the world beyond its walls. Deep within the armouries of Narin Hamiya, the elite commander Eldarion Fireblade drew his sword. Its blade was blacker than the Devil's soul, its darkened steel veined with the living flames of Hell itself. A crimson radiance spilled across the armoury walls, and for a moment, the shadows recoiled from its terrible light. Eldarion raised the weapon. “Vanguard! To the First Black Gate!” His warriors answered with the thunder of steel against shields. They were no green recruits, nor were they strangers to the horrors of the world. They had marched through the frozen northern wastes, where ancient dragons bred beneath mountains of ice. They had crossed the forsaken territories of Orkishnand and wrested the Jewels of the Phoenix from the vaults of its mighty treasury. They had faced monsters that would have driven lesser men to madness. They had returned victorious. But as Eldarion led them towards the First Black Gate, even the oldest among them could hear something beyond the walls that chilled their blood. The enemy was singing. By night the entire castle was armed and anti-siege machines were laid ready for action. Then it came. Hundreds of siege towers taller than a giant with orcs upon them came.Drums beating ,shields clanging and voices yelling.The defenders knew something.
